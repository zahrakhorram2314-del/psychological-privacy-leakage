# Conditions and Standardized Stimuli

## Purpose
Use the same fictional scenario and disclosure options in all four conditions. Do not ask participants to type real personal information.

## Fictional scenario
"You are trying a fictional AI study-planning assistant. It offers to tailor a sample weekly study plan using a fictional profile. You may choose which invented details to share. You can skip any item."

## Fictional profile cards
All details are invented and should not resemble participants' real lives.

| Item | Fictional detail | Predefined sensitivity |
|---|---|---|
| A | Preferred study format: short reading blocks | Low |
| B | Fictional weekly availability: weekday afternoons | Low |
| C | Fictional challenge: nervousness before exams | Moderate |
| D | Fictional family situation: a made-up disagreement at home | Higher |

For each card, show **Share this fictional detail** and **Skip**. No free-text field.

## Tone manipulation

### Warm / socially expressive
"Hi! I'm glad to help you shape a study plan. We can take this one step at a time. You can choose any fictional details below—or skip them. There's no need to share anything real or personal."

### Neutral / task-focused
"Study-plan setup. Select any fictional details below to generate a sample plan. Each item is optional. Do not enter real personal information."

The warm version must not imply friendship, confidentiality, exclusive understanding, emotional dependence, or guaranteed safety.

## Privacy cue manipulation

### Contextual notice present
"Privacy reminder: This is a fictional exercise. Do not provide real names, locations, school or workplace details, family information, health information, passwords, or private experiences. The choices below are optional. You can skip every item and continue."

### No additional contextual notice
Do not show the additional notice. The base scenario must still explain that all profile cards are fictional and optional. The experiment tests an additional reminder, not whether participants receive essential safety information.

## Four conditions
1. Warm tone + contextual notice.
2. Warm tone + no additional notice.
3. Neutral tone + contextual notice.
4. Neutral tone + no additional notice.

## Standardization checklist
- Same scenario, cards, order, buttons, and skip affordance.
- Same number of screens and approximate exposure time.
- No free-text fields.
- No live AI service, external API, analytics, or third-party logging.
- Random assignment and record the exact stimulus version.

## Debrief
"Thank you. This study examines how conversational tone and privacy reminders may influence choices in a fictional AI interaction. All profile details were invented. Please do not send the research team real personal or sensitive information. The study is exploratory; no single response means a person is careless or unsafe."
